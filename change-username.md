# Change Bot Username

Since you can't use UserBot, let's get a better username for your bot.

## Steps:

1. Message @BotFather
2. Send `/setusername`
3. Select your bot (Spinbattles bot)
4. Try these usernames (in order):
   - `spinbattles_support`
   - `spinbattles_contact`
   - `spinbattles_help`
   - `spinbattlesupport`
   - `contact_spinbattles`

## Then update your website:

```html
<a href="https://t.me/spinbattles_support" target="_blank">
   💬 Contact us on Telegram
</a>
```

This way customers message the bot directly, get auto-replies, and you get notified.
